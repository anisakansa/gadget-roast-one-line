My entry roasts the one gadget that never leaves my side: my wireless earbuds. The joke is simple — they hold on fine all day, then choose the exact middle of a sentence on a call to drop out, and announce their low battery in a whisper, as if they were spreading gossip about me. I wrote the line myself for this bounty; the program below just puts it on disk, unchanged.

## What the bounty asks for

[One-Line Roast: The Gadget You Use Every Day](https://make.fun/c/u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-roast-the-gadget-you-use-3d6aa610) wants a single original line, no more than 200 characters, poking fun at a gadget or app you use daily. Entry is free, the pot is $5, and the top three entries get paid.

## How to run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Running it drops the roast into `/output/entry.txt`: one line, 121 characters, and no trailing newline. Before writing anything, the program checks its own output against the gate — it bails out instead of writing if the line is under 1 character, over 200, split across lines, or padded with whitespace at either end.
